Setup

Get Day Focus working properly on iPhone and Mac in a few minutes.

⚡
Your check-in history syncs automatically between iPhone and Mac — no login needed. Every device that opens this URL shares the same data.
iPhone
1
Add to Home Screen
Open this page in Safari on your iPhone. Tap the Share button (box with arrow) at the bottom → scroll down → tap Add to Home Screen → name it Day Focus → tap Add.
The icon appears on your home screen like a native app, opens full-screen with no browser bar.
2
Download the 08:30 check-in alarm
AirDrop or email this to your iPhone, then open it — Calendar imports it as a daily recurring alarm at 08:30.
3
Download all medication reminders
ADHD meds (08:00), PrEP (12:00), and evening meds — lithium, olanzapine, promethazine (20:00). All daily, all with alerts.
4
Allow Calendar notifications
Settings → Calendar → Notifications → Allow Notifications — must be on for the alarms to fire.
Mac
5
Bookmark or pin in Safari
Open this URL in Safari on your Mac → press Cmd+D to bookmark it to your Favourites Bar, or right-click the tab → Pin Tab so it's always open.
6
Import the .ics files on Mac too
Double-click the downloaded files in your Downloads folder — Calendar imports them. Since you use iCloud Calendar, they appear on your iPhone automatically within seconds.

GitHub Pages hosting (recommended)
★
Host this as your own permanent URL
1. Go to github.com and create a free account if you don't have one.
2. Create a new repository named dayfocus.
3. Upload the index.html file you downloaded from Claude.
4. Go to repo Settings → Pages → Source: main branch → Save.
5. Your app is live at https://yourusername.github.io/dayfocus
6. Add that URL to your iPhone home screen — it's yours forever, independent of Claude.
